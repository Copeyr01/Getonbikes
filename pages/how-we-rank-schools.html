How we rank schools

What decides the order you see results in — today, and once reviews launch.

Today: no ranking algorithm, because there's nothing to rank yet

There are no rider reviews on Upshift yet, so there's no "best school" for us to have worked out. Search results are ordered by distance when you search a postcode, or shown in a fixed order otherwise. No school is placed higher because of a relationship with us — there isn't a relationship to have, since none of them pay us anything today.

Once reviews launch

Rankings will be based on genuine rider reviews — average rating and review count — submitted by people who've actually trained with a school. We won't hand-edit that order to favour one school over another.

Sponsored placements: what will and won't change

We expect to offer sponsored placements in future as a way to fund the site — clearly labelled as sponsored, and shown separately from organic, review-based results. A sponsored slot will never let a school outrank a better-reviewed competitor in the organic list, and it will never hide or bury genuine reviews. Rankings are never for sale — only visibility alongside them is.

Removing reviews

Once review submission is live, we'll remove reviews that are fake, abusive, or clearly not about a genuine training experience. We won't remove a review just because it's negative.

Questions

If something about a listing looks off, or you think a review breaks these rules, get in touch.
