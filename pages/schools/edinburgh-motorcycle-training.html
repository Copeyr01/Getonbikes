About

Edinburgh Motorcycle Training is an independent school based in Straiton, on the southern edge of the city, offering CBT, A1, A2 and full licence (DAS) courses. Training starts at a dedicated off-road site before moving on to Edinburgh's roads. Complete beginners with no motorcycling experience are asked to complete a short Introduction to Motorcycling session before booking CBT.

Courses offered

CBT Compulsory Basic Training

Runs on weekdays and weekends (weekend and bank holiday courses priced slightly higher). Required before riding on the road on a provisional licence. Complete beginners need an Introduction to Motorcycling session first.

A1 Light motorcycle licence

Priced hourly rather than as a fixed course — CBT required first.

A2 Restricted licence

Priced hourly rather than as a fixed course — CBT required first.

Full A Direct Access (DAS)

Full, unrestricted motorcycle licence training — a 4-day or 5-day course, including the pre-DAS assessment.

CBT and Full A (DAS) are fixed-price courses. A1 and A2 training is priced hourly (2 students to an instructor, or 1-to-1) plus test fees — contact the school for a cost estimate.
