About

Two Wheels is a Honda Approved School of Motorcycling based on Peffermill Road, training riders across Edinburgh and the Lothians since 1991. They run CBT weekly and intensive Direct Access (DAS) courses, with A1 and A2 training as required.

Courses offered

CBT Compulsory Basic Training

Required before riding on the road on a provisional licence. Runs weekly.

A1 Light motorcycle licence

For riders aged 17+, up to 125cc — requires CBT and a theory test pass first.

A2 Restricted licence

For riders aged 19+, restricted to 35kW (47bhp).

Full A Direct Access (DAS)

Intensive Category A courses running every week for riders aged 24+.

Prices are provided by the school and can change — confirm the exact cost when you book.
